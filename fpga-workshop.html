About

A hands-on workshop on the fundamentals of hardware acceleration, organised by the IEEE CASS Student Branch Chapter, IIT Guwahati (SBC61571I), in collaboration with the IEEE Kolkata Section Chapter, CAS04-Guwahati (CH11218), and supported by the IEEE Circuits and Systems Society.

The workshop is designed to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge of digital systems and their implementation on real hardware. Participants learn the basics of Verilog HDL and RTL design, then design a simple computational unit such as a multiply-accumulate (MAC) unit, an FIR filter or a matrix multiplication block, and take it all the way onto an FPGA development board.

Along the way, participants see how an algorithm becomes real hardware, from the idea on paper to a working design running on an FPGA, and get a hands-on feel for VLSI design, digital hardware, embedded systems and AI acceleration. It is a starting point for a final-year project, research, or a career in the industry.

What you will do

Sessions start from the basics and build up step by step to a working design on an FPGA board. The detailed schedule will be announced soon.

  1. Hardware acceleration basics

    Why and how algorithms are accelerated in hardware.

  2. Verilog HDL and RTL design

    Writing synthesisable Verilog and thinking at the register-transfer level.

  3. Design a computational unit

    A MAC unit, an FIR filter or a matrix multiplication block.

  4. Simulate and verify

    Testbenches and simulation to check the design works.

  5. Synthesise and analyse

    Synthesis with FPGA design tools and a look at hardware resource utilisation.

  6. Run it on an FPGA board

    Implement the design on a development board and see it work.
