About us

The IEEE Circuits and Systems Society Student Branch Chapter at IIT Guwahati brings together students, research scholars and faculty working on circuits, systems, VLSI and related areas. We organise invited lectures, workshops and hands-on sessions, and work alongside the IEEE CAS Kolkata Section Chapter, CAS04-Guwahati (CH11218), to strengthen the circuits-and-systems community in North-East India.

The IEEE Circuits and Systems Society (CASS) is the leading professional organisation advancing the theory, analysis, design, tools and implementation of circuits and systems, spanning signal and information processing, architectures and their real-world applications. It connects engineers, researchers and students worldwide through publications, conferences and career-building resources.

  • Founded in 1949One of IEEE's founding technical societies, shaping the field for more than 75 years.
  • 10 regions worldwideChapters across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia and the Pacific.
  • A global, interdisciplinary communityEngineers, researchers and scientists collaborating for the benefit of humanity.
